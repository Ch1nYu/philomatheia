# Acknowledgments and references

Philomatheia combines an Agent Skill engineering pattern with a general learning-state model. The references below influenced different parts of the design; their inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation, or evidence that Philomatheia reproduces the educational outcomes of the cited work.

## Direct project inspiration

- [AI Engineering from Scratch](https://github.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch) by [Rohit Ghumare](https://github.com/rohitg00), especially its interactive `learn` workflow: prior-knowledge recall, problem framing, building from scratch, production comparison, quiz, and persisted course progress. The upstream repository is MIT-licensed. Philomatheia does not bundle or reproduce its lessons, quiz bank, code, or fixed curriculum.
- [Skill 設計的一些注意要點](https://www.chongwenz.cn/tech/AI/ai-skill-01/) by ChongWen, particularly its explanation of progressive disclosure across `SKILL.md`, `references/`, `scripts/`, and `assets/`, plus deterministic scripts and explicit trigger boundaries. The article is referenced by link and paraphrase only; its text and images are not redistributed here.
- [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills), the official OpenAI documentation for Agent Skill structure, local discovery, invocation, and distribution.
- [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/), the open format used for the skill package.

## Related learning-science foundations

The learning contract is consistent with established findings and frameworks around retrieval, spacing, mastery, and cognitive load. These references motivate design choices; they do not validate this implementation as an educational intervention.

- Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). [Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x).
- Cepeda, N. J., Pashler, H., Vul, E., Wixted, J. T., & Rohrer, D. (2006). [Distributed Practice in Verbal Recall Tasks: A Review and Quantitative Synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354).
- Bloom, B. S. (1968). [Learning for Mastery](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED053419).
- Sweller, J. (1988). [Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1202_4).
