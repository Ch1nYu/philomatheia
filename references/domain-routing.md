# Domain routing

Use this reference to adapt the graph, interaction, evidence, and artifacts to the subject. Keep the common mastery standard while changing what counts as valid performance.

## Technical and programming

- Map concepts, APIs, runtime behavior, debugging strategies, and system relationships.
- Move from a concrete input/output trace to pseudo code, then implementation.
- Require executable behavior, tests, or observable runtime evidence for application claims.
- Distinguish a successful demo from a test suite and a test suite from production validation.

## Mathematics and formal subjects

- Ground symbols in a concrete table, diagram, quantity, or worked case before manipulating them.
- Track definitions, representations, lemmas, procedures, and proof strategies as different node kinds.
- Use prediction, derivation, counterexample, proof repair, and transfer to changed assumptions as evidence.
- Do not accept memorized formula substitution as conceptual transfer.

## Conceptual, humanities, and research topics

- Map claims, schools, evidence, methods, context, contrasts, and unresolved debates.
- Prefer primary texts or original research when interpretation depends on them.
- Use comparison, argument reconstruction, source criticism, application to a new case, and teach-back as evidence.
- Separate accurate representation of a position from agreement with it.

## Language learning

- Map comprehension, production, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, register, and communicative situations.
- Sample both recognition and production; passive recognition does not establish active use.
- Use spaced retrieval, contrasts, constrained production, correction, and changed-context communication.
- Treat pronunciation verification as limited unless suitable audio or a competent evaluator is available.

## Creative and practical skills

- Map technique, judgment, workflow, feedback, and safety prerequisites.
- Use progressive attempts and artifacts, with criteria appropriate to the medium.
- Preserve the learner's intent instead of imposing a single aesthetic standard.
- Do not claim physical execution, safety, or real-world performance that was not observed.

## Artifact decision

Require a durable artifact when the goal is applied, central, or benefits from external inspection. Use lighter evidence for supporting concepts. Define the artifact and its verification criterion before treating production as mastery.
