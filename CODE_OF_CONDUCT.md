# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, patience, and intellectual honesty. Discuss ideas and observable behavior without attacking people. Make space for different experience levels, languages, abilities, and learning needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private learner data, and sustained disruption of project discussion.

## Learning-specific expectations

- Do not shame learners for mistakes, pace, disability, language, or prior knowledge.
- Do not present unverified educational, medical, psychological, or safety claims as established fact.
- Do not publish another person's learning state, artifacts, or conversations without permission.
- Critique mastery claims by examining evidence and task design.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's contact or private reporting features. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
